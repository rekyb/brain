# Kingdom Come: Deliverance — Perks & Early Game

> [!info] Scope
> **Kingdom Come: Deliverance (2018)** — the first game, not KCD2, which uses different skill trees and
> perk names. Perk **levels and effects** are verified against
> [The Kingdom Come: Deliverance Wiki](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks).
> **Ratings (★) are opinion** and flagged as such throughout.

**How to use this note**

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Start here** | The first ten hours: saving, Rattay checklist, combat, money, survival. |
| **2. How perks work** | Perk points, exclusivity, hidden perks, the rating scale. |
| **3–5. Perk tables** | Every perk in the game — level, effect, rating, verdict — by tree. |

> [!abstract] Top picks
> **Master Strike** (hidden, trained by Captain Bernard) is the single strongest unlock in the game —
> get it in your first hours. After that: **Mule I–III** (Strength), **Tight Grip** and **Heavy Swing**
> (Strength), **Routine I + II** (Alchemy), **Revenant** or **Last Gasp** (Vitality 12, exclusive),
> **Against All Odds** (Warfare 12) and **Cartographer** (Reading 14).
>
> Most over-rated perk: **Luck of the Drunk**. It does not unlock Very Hard locks — it makes lockpicking
> 30% easier *and 30% noisier*, and only while drunk.

## 1. Start here — the first ten hours

Henry begins deliberately incompetent: no horse, no money, no combat training. Nearly everything
improves once the main quest reaches **Rattay**.

### Saving

- There is **no quicksave**. The game saves when you sleep at least an hour in a bed you **own or have
  rented**, use a **bathhouse** bed (~30 groschen, which also heals wounds and washes off blood), drink
  **Saviour Schnapps**, or use **Save and Quit**.
- Saves rotate through a small number of slots, so an old save is not a permanent checkpoint. Sleep
  before anything risky: a duel, a night burglary, a timed quest.
- **Saviour Schnapps** is the game's real quality-of-life unlock. Recipe: **wine** base, **1 nettle**,
  **2 belladonna**. It's already in your book but unreadable until you can read. Buy out every
  apothecary early, then brew in bulk.

### The Rattay checklist

- **[Train Hard, Fight Easy](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Train_Hard,_Fight_Easy)** —
  Captain Bernard at the training grounds, after *Awakening* and entering Radzig's service. Pays 70
  groschen; beat Hans Capon at both the archery contest and the duel to win his hunting bow.
- **Go back to Bernard afterwards.** Training unlocks combos and, at weapon skill 3+, **Master Strike**
  — for all weapon types at once. Sparring is also the only zero-risk way to grind combat skills.
- **Bow training** with Berthold and Nicholas opens at the same time.
- **[The Good Thief](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Good_Thief)** — from Miller Peshek.
  Clears your 100 groschen of debt, introduces lockpicking, turns the **millers into fences** and opens
  the *Thick as Thieves* jobs. Peshek also trains **Lockpicking and Pickpocketing**.
- **[Mightier than the Sword](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Reading)** — until it's done
  every book is literally gibberish, which blocks the Schnapps recipe and all stat-boosting books.

### Combat

- **Stamina is your real health bar.** Attacks and blocks both drain it; at zero, blows land straight on
  Health. Health does **not** regenerate during a fight.
- **Master Strike beats combos.** Competent opponents interrupt combos. Block on the green shield cue
  and let the automatic counter work.
- **Never fight a group in the open.** Back into a doorway or narrow path so they come one at a time.
  Running away is a tactic, not a failure.
- **Match weapon to armour**: blunt (mace, axe, hammer) against plate; slashing and stabbing (sword,
  sabre) against light armour. A sword against full plate is close to useless.
- **Clinches**: kick to initiate, then hammer attack to overpower — the winner gets a free hit.
- Bandage **bleeding** the moment a fight ends and treat fractures; untreated injuries cap your Health.

### Money

- **Herbalism is the safest early income** and levels a skill at the same time. Nettles do double duty:
  a Schnapps ingredient, and 100 of them grant the hidden *Resistance* perk (+2 Vitality).
- **Loot selectively.** Bandit and Cuman gear is heavy and mostly rusty; take rings, coin and intact
  weapons. Over the weight limit you cannot sprint and fight noticeably worse.
- **Stolen goods are flagged.** Sell to the miller fences (Peshek, Woyzeck, Simon), or stash them in a
  chest for one to two in-game weeks until the flag clears.
- **Traders deal only in their own category and have limited purses** — split big hauls across towns.
- **Repair before selling.** Condition drives price directly.

### Survival, stats and gear upkeep

- Keep hunger near 100% without overeating, and avoid food below roughly 50% freshness.
- Sleep in decent beds; poor bedding gives poor rest (*Wanderer* inverts this if you live rough).
- **Wash.** Blood and grime gut Charisma and make you recognisable after a crime. Bathhouses fix it all
  at once.
- **Repair everything.** Any smithy's grindstone sharpens weapons cheaply; clothing and armour need
  repair kits. Damaged gear loses effectiveness proportionally.
- Gear has **stat requirements** — armour you're too weak for penalises you.
- Use your **horse's saddlebags** as mobile storage until Mule I–III fixes carry weight properly.

### Skill grinding

Skills rise through **use**, not quest completion: spar with Bernard, pick every herb you pass, pick
every unlocked chest, ride everywhere instead of fast-travelling. Trainers are expensive early — self-
train first and spend groschen on gear, Schnapps and bribes.

## 2. How perks work

- A perk's listed level is the **minimum stat or skill level** needed to select it.
- Perk points arrive at **fixed thresholds** per tree (Defence at 4/8/12/16/20; Maintenance at
  3/6/9/12/15/18/20; Main Level every second level). You cannot take everything — most tiers force a
  choice, and several of the strongest perks collide on the *same* tier. Conflicts are flagged below.
- Some perks are **mutually exclusive** by design: Burgher/Savage, Highborn/Lowborn, Marathon
  Man/Sprinter, Beer Bibber/Wine Bibber, Racehorse/Heavy Pony Duty.
- A few are **hidden perks** granted by actions rather than level-ups: Master Strike, Cuman Killer,
  Resistance, Relieved, Gamekeeper Rules the Woods.

### Rating scale

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ★★★ | **Recommend** — usually the best use of the point in its tier. |
| ★★ | **Leftover** — situational, or a fair consolation when the tier offers nothing better. |
| ★ | **Do not pick** — weak, self-defeating, or made redundant by something else. |

In the tables below, **Effect is sourced**; **★ and Verdict are opinion**. A ★ perk for a knight is
sometimes ★★★ for a thief — those cases are called out.

### Bow, Unarmed and Halberd

**No perks exist for these in the vanilla game** — skill level alone reduces sway and Stamina cost and
increases zoom. Mods such as *Blood and Iron* add trees for all three. The bow-adjacent perks live
elsewhere: **Drinking Habit** (Drinking 4, +2 Bow while drunk) and **Knight** (Horsemanship 10).
