# App idea candidates — 22 problems

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Brainstormed list of 22 problem areas for a from-scratch solo app, biased toward React Native/Expo skills (offline-first, sync queue, notifications). Each entry has App Store seed keywords for the research workflow in [[Free App Store Keyword Research]]. **Decision: deep-diving #14 (book tracker) first** — see [[book-tracker]].

## Round 1

1. **Shift workers can't use normal habit/routine apps** — rotating schedules break every "daily" model. Seeds: `shift work sleep`, `rotating schedule`, `nurse schedule`, `shift planner`
2. **Subscription creep** — manual-entry, offline, renewal-reminder tracker; incumbents are US-centric and bank-linked. Seeds: `subscription tracker`, `cancel subscriptions`, `bill reminder`
3. **Group expense splitting** — Splitwise paywalled core features; loud 1★ anger. Offline-first + sync-queue architecture fits. Seeds: `split bills`, `splitwise alternative`, `group expenses`
4. **Medication/supplement adherence** — simple check-off + smart reminders; incumbents are hospital-grade or ugly. Seeds: `pill reminder`, `medication tracker`, `supplement tracker`
5. **Home inventory** — warranties, serials, receipts; local-first + photos + search. Seeds: `home inventory`, `warranty tracker`, `receipt organizer`
6. **Freelance mobile time tracking** — phone-native timer + client tags + CSV export, one-time price. Seeds: `time tracker freelance`, `billable hours`, `work hours log`
7. **Loaned items / borrowed money between friends** — tiny scope, notification-driven. Seeds: `lend tracker`, `borrowed items`, `IOU`
8. **Language-learning consistency layer** — meta streak/reminder tracker for self-directed learners (Anki + YouTube + tutoring). Adjacent to edtech day job. Seeds: `study streak`, `study planner`, `learning habit`
9. **Prayer/fasting/religious routine tracking** — huge loyal niches (Muslim prayer + Ramadan, Bible plans); incumbents ad-stuffed; offline + notifications is the whole product. Seeds: `prayer tracker`, `fasting tracker`, `quran reading plan`
10. **Vehicle maintenance logs** — esp. motorbikes in SEA; incumbents car-centric and Western. Seeds: `motorcycle maintenance`, `car service log`, `fuel tracker`

## Round 2

11. **Plant care guesswork** — watering reminders per plant/season; Planta paywall anger. Seeds: `plant watering reminder`, `plant care`
12. **Baby/newborn logging** — feeds/diapers/sleep; high willingness to pay, ~12-month churn but constant new users; 3am offline reliability. Seeds: `baby feeding tracker`, `newborn log`
13. **Gym progress in the Notes app** — fast set/rep/weight entry; Strong/Hevy paywall routines; niche variants (calisthenics, rehab). Seeds: `workout log`, `gym tracker`
14. **Reading tracker** ⭐ *(chosen — see [[book-tracker]])* — Goodreads ancient, StoryGraph web-first; fast offline log + streaks + yearly goal; BookTok audience. Seeds: `reading tracker`, `book log`, `reading streak`, `goodreads alternative`
15. **Chore rotation for households** — shared rotation + nudges; small social graph (2–5), sync-queue fits. Seeds: `chores app`, `roommate chores`
16. **Private cycle tracking** — "data never leaves the device" as the pitch; real distrust of incumbents. Seeds: `period tracker private`, `cycle tracker offline`
17. **Small landlord / kos-kosan rent management** — who paid, contract ends, meter readings; underserved in local language; B2B-ish pricing. Seeds: `rent tracker landlord`, `kos management`
18. **Wardrobe / cost-per-wear** — capsule-wardrobe communities; incumbents clunky with forced accounts. Seeds: `closet organizer`, `outfit planner`
19. **Specific interval timers** — boxing, physio holds, tea; trivially buildable, very specific search phrases. Seeds: `boxing timer`, `stretching timer`
20. **Intentional friction for screen time** — "confirm before opening Instagram"; one sec/Opal prove willingness to pay; sub + battery complaints. Seeds: `app blocker`, `stop scrolling`
21. **Wedding planning at guest-list-and-budget scale** — big-wedding cultures (SEA); incumbents are US vendor marketplaces. Seeds: `wedding budget`, `guest list`
22. **Document expiry anxiety** — passports, visas, licenses, certs; date + escalating reminders + photo. Tiny app, sharp pain. Seeds: `document expiry reminder`, `visa reminder`

## Cross-cutting patterns

Strongest solo-dev plays: **(a)** incumbent paywall anger (#3, #11, #13, #14), **(b)** privacy as the product (#16), **(c)** local-market gaps big players ignore (#9, #17, #21). See [[Finding App Ideas]].
